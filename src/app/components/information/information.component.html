Tournament Buy-In Details

  • Stroke Play Tournament Entry: $20 to participate in the net/gross tournament.
    • $1 goes toward Hole In One Pot
    • $1 goes toward End Of Year Pot
    • Remaining pot goes to top 5 net and top 5 gross scores.
  • Optional Skins Game: Additional $20 entry fee (optional).
    • $10 goes toward Net Skins pool.
    • $10 goes toward Gross Skins pool.

How Points Are Accumulated

  • End of Year Tourney Entry: The top 15 point earners for the year qualify. The tourney is scheduled for late November to early December, with the exact date to be finalized soon.
  • Point Earnings:
    • You are guaranteed 10 points for playing in any event.
    • Net and Gross Rankings:
      • 1st place (including ties): +10 points for winning net or gross.
      • 2nd place: +9 points.
      • 3rd place: +8 points.
      • 4th to 10th place: Points decrease by 1 per rank (e.g., 7 points for 4th, 6 for 5th, down to 1 for 10th).
    • Net vs Gross Rule: You only get points for your better finish (net or gross). For example, if you place 8th in net (3 points) and 3rd in gross (8 points), you’ll earn the 8 points for 3rd gross—not both.

Club Championship

  • Qualification: Anyone who has played in at least 2 events through out the year will be invited to participate in the club championship.
  • Date: 2 Day event. Scheduled for November 22 and 23.
  • First day you may pick your group. Second day tee times will be based on first round scores.

End of Year Tournament

  • Qualification: The top 20 point earners for the year will be invited to participate in the end of year tournament.
  • Enhanced Payouts: This special tournament features increased payouts compared to regular weekly events.
  • Date: Scheduled for December 7.
