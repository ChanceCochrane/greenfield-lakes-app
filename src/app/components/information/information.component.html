Club Rules

  • All play shall follow USGA rules
  • Local Rule: Railway-Tie rule, Read Scorecard for any questions
  • Tee box selection is determined by age and handicap: players whose age and handicap total 65 or more may play from Green tees (if younger than 65 Yellow tee requirement)
  • Alternatively, players with a handicap over 18 are eligible to play from Green tees
  • Groups must submit a scorecard at the conclusion of the round
  • Live scoring during the round is strongly encouraged to enhance the tournament experience
  • Flights will be established based on number of players and handicap
  • Hole In One Pot can only be won by an active member of the Greenfield Lakes Golf League

Tournament Details

  • Tournament Entry: $20 to participate in any tournament.
    • $1 goes toward Hole In One Pot
    • $1 goes toward Player Of The Year
    • $5 goes toward closest to pin/long drive pot
    • Remaining pot goes to pay out top gross and net scores
  • Optional Skins Game: Additional $20 entry fee (optional).
    • $10 goes toward Net Skins pool.
    • $10 goes toward Gross Skins pool.

How Points Are Accumulated For Player Of The Year

  • Player Of The Year Tourney Entry: The top 20 point earners for the year qualify. The tourney is scheduled to run the last 3 events of the year starting November 20.
  • Point Earnings:
    • You are guaranteed 10 points for playing in any event.
    • Rankings and Points will be awarded per flight if there are any flights
    • Net and Gross Rankings:
      • 1st place (including ties): +10 points for winning net or gross.
      • 2nd place: +9 points.
      • 3rd place: +8 points.
      • 4th to 10th place: Points decrease by 1 per rank (e.g., 7 points for 4th, 6 for 5th, down to 1 for 10th).
    • Net vs Gross Rule: You only get points for your better finish (net or gross). For example, if you place 8th in net (3 points) and 3rd in gross (8 points), you'll earn the 8 points for 3rd gross—not both.
    • The three majors (Greenfield Major, 2-Man Ryder Cup, and Fun Major) will award triple points.

Club Championship

  • Qualification: Anyone who has played in at least 3 events through out the year will be invited to participate in the club championship.
  • Date: 1 Day event. Scheduled for December 6.
  • We will crown both a Net and Gross Club Champion.

Player of The Year Tournament

  • Qualification: The top 20 point earners for the year will be invited to participate in the end of year tournament.
  • Date: Scheduled to start November 22 and end December 20.
  • Format: 1v1 Net Stroke Play Single Elimination
