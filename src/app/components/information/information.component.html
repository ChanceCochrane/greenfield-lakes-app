How Points Are Accumulated

  • End of Year Tourney Entry: The top 15 point earners for the year qualify. The tourney is scheduled for late November to early December, with the exact date to be finalized soon.
  • Point Earnings:
    • You are guaranteed 10 points for playing in any event.
    • Win a skin: Awarded 5 points.
    • Win net outright: Awarded 15 additional points.
    • Second place: Awarded 14 points.
    • Third place: Awarded 13 points.
    • Places 4th to 15th: Awarded points decreasing by 1 each (e.g., 12 for 4th, 11 for 5th, down to 1 for 15th).
  • Future Plans: We plan to introduce flights and distribute points based on flights once we average 40 people per event. Until then, with 10-15 people per flight, it doesn’t make sense.
